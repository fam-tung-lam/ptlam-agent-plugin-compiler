# Usability and M3 Expressive

Use M3 Expressive for every artifact to improve comprehension, hierarchy, and
emotional clarity. Combine vibrant role colors, contrasting shapes, purposeful
size, containment, flexible type, and motion physics around the current task.

- Give the primary action or current state the strongest visual voice.
- Use shape changes to clarify selection, expansion, or progress.
- Use motion to preserve spatial continuity and show cause and effect.
- Keep expressive movement interruptible and fully reduced by user preference.
- Keep familiar component anatomy and predictable interaction behavior.
- Test whether emphasis helps people find, understand, and complete the task.
- Limit each page or stage to one primary task, and reserve hero moments for
  short success, progress, or other high-value transitions.
- Use size, contrast, and containment to create hierarchy; avoid making several
  regions equally loud.

The single expressive system changes intensity with context but never switches
to a classic version. Preserve familiar interaction patterns, visible text
labels, usability, accessibility, and cross-platform adaptation. The research
basis spans 46 studies with more than 18,000 participants and identifies color,
shape, size, motion, and containment as the fundamental dimensions.
